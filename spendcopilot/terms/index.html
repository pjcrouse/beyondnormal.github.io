2. What the service is

SpendCopilot is an iOS application, supported by a backend service, that lets you connect financial accounts, record spending, and see an organized view of your financial position. It is operated by Beyond Normal.

The numbers SpendCopilot shows are derived from what you enter and from data supplied by your financial institutions through Plaid. Institution data can be delayed, incomplete, or later corrected by the institution itself. SpendCopilot is a view onto that data, not the system of record — your bank is.
